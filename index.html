Voice education

Your Voice is Yours.

Pitch Volume Resonance Respiratory Intonation

Voice is what makes each of us unique and individual. The larynx and vocal cords are the most under-rated and under-considered anatomical structures in the human body. Until we lose our voice or don't have access to it anymore or our voice is what is causing us to feel less confident than we deserve to be.

Pitch, Volume, Resonance, Respiratory, Intonation: All of these anatomical structures and physiological responses are malleable and we do have more control over everything that makes up "voice" than we think. Learn to have increased control and flexibility over your voice. Gain Confidence in your voice. Find your authentic, unique and individual voice that best aligns with your identity.
